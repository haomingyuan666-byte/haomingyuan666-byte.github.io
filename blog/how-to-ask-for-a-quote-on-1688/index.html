How to Ask a 1688 Seller for a Complete-Unit Quote

November 16, 2026

If you are working out how to ask for a quote on 1688, write a message the seller can answer line by line. Name the exact configuration, state the quantities, and ask for a written price per complete unit at each quantity. Ask the seller to list the included items and price any excluded items separately. That gives you a reply you can read without guessing what the number buys.

Hao Studio used this approach for a 3.2L pet drinking fountain made of 304 stainless steel. The ¥29.80 card price corresponded to an accessory or smaller-capacity option. Once the configuration and complete-unit scope were clear, the confirmed prices were ¥108 per unit at 200 units and ¥106 per unit at 500 units. Keep the listing price and the written quote with their respective scopes when you record the reply.

Name the configuration in the message

Begin with a line that identifies the exact item you want priced. For the fountain, state the capacity and material together, then name the complete fountain as the unit of purchase. Include the listing reference or selected option so the seller can find the item, and repeat the configuration in text. This makes the inquiry usable when the seller replies from a different device or forwards it to someone else who may see the message without the selected option.

Ask the seller to repeat the model and configuration in the reply. If they suggest a different capacity or material, ask them to label that price as an alternative and answer the original request if they can. In your record, attach the new price to the configuration the seller named. A price for an alternative is useful when you can see exactly what would change if you bought it.

State each order quantity on its own line

Give the seller a price line to fill in for each order size you are considering. For the fountain, the inquiry asked about 200 units and 500 units. Put the quantity next to the requested unit price so a reply can be read without looking back through the chat. Specify that the quantity refers to complete fountains. This helps avoid a reply that counts individually priced accessories toward the requested units.

Ask the seller to state which quantity applies if they reply with a single price. If a tier is unavailable, record that condition and any alternative they actually offer. Leave the price field blank for unanswered tiers. That keeps the unanswered quantity visible when you later compare the reply with your buying plan or ask the seller to price a revised order.

Request a written price per complete unit

Ask for a written yuan amount per complete fountain at each stated quantity. Use the words “per complete fountain” in the question and ask the seller to confirm that scope in their reply. A bare figure can otherwise refer to the selected listing option, a component, or a different quantity. If you need an order total for approval, ask the seller to label it separately so it cannot be confused with the per-unit amount.

Ask what is included with the unit

Name the components and packaging you need the seller to confirm. In the fountain case, the inquiry covered the complete fountain, the color box, the filter, and whether the pump could be purchased separately. Ask whether the quoted unit includes the pump and filter and whether the color box is included in the same price. These are questions about scope. Keep a field open if the seller confirms the unit price but leaves a component or the packaging unanswered.

For a reply that says “standard packaging,” ask whether that means a color box for this configuration. If the seller lists an included filter, preserve their wording so someone preparing the order can check what was agreed. Keeping the original language is especially helpful when a translated product name can refer to either a component or a complete assembly.

Ask for separate prices on excluded items

Give the seller a specific place to list anything billed separately. Ask for the item name, its unit price, and when that price applies. Also ask whether the pump can be purchased on its own, and request a separate quote if available. Keep replacement parts, packaging charges, and other separately stated items on their own lines in your notes. The price of a pump bought separately belongs with that item even if the same message contains the price of the complete fountain.

Use this message structure

Use the following as the message itself and replace the configuration and quantities when you have a different inquiry. Give the seller room to answer each line. The requested amounts are left open for the seller to fill in.

  • Hello, please quote the complete 3.2L pet drinking fountain made of 304 stainless steel from the listing I am referencing. Please confirm that this is the configuration you are pricing.
  • For an order of 200 complete fountains, what is the price in yuan per complete unit?
  • For an order of 500 complete fountains, what is the price in yuan per complete unit?
  • Please reply in writing and confirm what ships with each quoted unit. Are the color box, filter, and pump included in that price?
  • Please list any items charged separately and give the price for each. Can the pump be purchased on its own? If so, please quote it separately.

Save the reply with its scope status

Keep the seller's original message with the listing reference, the requested configuration, the date, and the quantity tier. In your own notes, mark the scope as confirmed only when the seller identifies what the amount covers. Record any unanswered items next to that status. If a later message answers a packaging question or revises the quote, attach it to the original and note which part of the record changed. That creates a trail someone else can use to check the written terms before placing an order.

When you pass the quote to the person preparing the order, keep the scope status beside the unit price. A confirmed price can travel with an open packaging question. Name that open item explicitly so it can be resolved before anyone copies the quoted price into a purchase request that assumes the packaging is included.

Some sellers in the fountain inquiry replied only with an emoji or a contact card. For a reply like that, follow up with the specific line you need answered, such as whether the price covers the complete fountain at the requested quantity. Record the acknowledgment and keep the scope open while you wait for a written answer. When joining a conversation in another channel, resend the relevant configuration and quantity so the next reply still has a clear reference.
